# 19th Amendment: 1920

**Setting:** Chicago, during the height of the Roaring Twenties. The city is filled with jazz clubs, grand architecture, and political intrigue.

**Concept:** A psychological thriller and exploration game where the protagonist walks the neon-lit streets of Chicago in search of his missing wife. As the investigation deepens, he uncovers clues pointing toward an alternate reality where Prohibition was never enacted—or perhaps, he is simply losing his mind.

**Details:** Developed as part of a Steam bundle in two months.
